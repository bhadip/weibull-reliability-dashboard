
## 12. Sleep & keepalive (added 2026-08-24)

- Streamlit Cloud free tier sleeps on inactivity; Zzzz page cannot be woken from the iframe embed.
- Wake via direct URL: https://bcore-dashboard.streamlit.app
- psth1 cron pings the direct URL every 10 min to prevent sleep (crontab -l to inspect).
